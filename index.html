01. About me

I'm a PhD researcher at Universidad Miguel Hernández de Elche (Spain), working on connected automated vehicles — where robotics, wireless communication and artificial intelligence meet on the road.

My research lives at the intersection of three worlds: multisensor & cooperative perception for vehicles that see beyond their own sensors, LLM-based reasoning that gives machines the ability to explain and decide, and MLOps/LLMOps practices that turn research prototypes into reliable, reproducible production systems.

I come from an Electronics Engineering (BSc) and Telecommunication Engineering (MSc) background, and my goal is simple: convert cutting-edge research into real, scalable, reliable systems for the robotics, mobility and autonomous-systems industry.
